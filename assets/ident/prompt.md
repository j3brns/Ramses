# Sundial wireframe ident prompt history

## Intent

Create an original README ident for Ramses: a quiet, vector-like heating-programmer metaphor. It uses the broad language of early wireframe arcade instruments—phosphor line art, horizon grid and an abstract observation window—without copying a specific game's screen, logo, vehicle, weapon or interface.

## Prompt v1

> Wide README ident banner for an open-source Home Assistant heating-programmer project. An original, refined retro vector-arcade instrument illustration; deep midnight-navy to black field; a shallow trapezoidal observation window; an elegant classical sundial on a sparse horizon grid; a warm amber sun on a dawn-to-dusk arc; a sundial shadow aligned with clock and weekly-programme ticks; subtle abstract cyan zone/setpoint traces at the extreme edges. Crisp luminous vector-like line art, restrained, technical and calm. No text, numerals, logos, watermarks, tank, weapons, human, dashboard screenshot or photorealism.

**Review:** the concept read clearly, but the sun sat at the top of its arc and the cyan edge marks felt decorative rather than programmable.

## Prompt v2 — selected

> Refine the README ident while preserving the central wireframe sundial, trapezoidal observation viewport, dark navy field, horizon grid, pale phosphor-green line art and single amber sun. Move the sun to the left third of the trajectory and show its clean path continuing through midday toward dusk, so motion is immediately legible. Replace decorative cyan corner marks with two restrained symmetric schedule-step traces. Make the sundial face and shadow especially clear at small README-banner scale. Keep it panoramic, serene, premium and sparse. Do not add text, numerals, logos, game UI details, vehicles, weapons or photorealistic elements.

**Review:** selected after computer-use preview. At banner scale it preserves the sundial, moving sun and schedule tick ring; the heating-programmer cue remains legible without becoming a dashboard imitation.

## Prompt v3

> Preserve the v2 composition, but reduce viewport-frame weight, simplify the sundial ring and make the gnomon’s cast shadow more obvious.

**Review:** a valid alternative with a clearer shadow, but its simpler tick ring weakens the schedule/programmer metaphor. Retained for comparison, not selected.

## Acceptance criteria for future revisions

1. Recognisable at README-banner scale: sundial, sun path and time/schedule cue remain distinct.
2. Original high-level wireframe arcade influence only; no identifiable game assets or trademarks.
3. High contrast on a dark background; no text required to understand the idea.
4. Avoid product UI imitation, literal tank imagery, weapons and photorealism.
5. Preserve prompt, version, visual review result and selection rationale in this directory.
